# Gabriel Pollard — The illuminated manuscript

User-selected direction, 31 October 2026. Guidance combined from the attached frontend-inspiration SKILL.md, its library.md, and the installed inferen-sh landing-page-design skill.

## Art direction

Midnight ink #101a24, parchment #f3eddf, antique gold #c4a16b, pale gold #dfc79e, and brown #885e36. Bodoni Moda for the literary display; Manrope for reading and navigation. The source book's darkness-to-light imagery drives the visual metaphor.

The opening uses a custom CSS book object, visible spine/page edges, a narrow diagonal ray and a quiet circular construction line. Below it, generous book spreads, folio rules, an inset paper signup, a supplied author portrait and a Roman-numeral forthcoming-book detail vary the composition.

## Interaction system

One self-hosted GSAP core provides finite reveal animation. The book responds subtly to fine pointers; reduced-motion disables large animation. The three chapter themes share a proper tablist with Arrow/Home/End keyboard behavior. A mobile chapter shortcut appears after the hero and hides around the signup and footer. Native scrolling is retained.
