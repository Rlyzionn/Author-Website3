Gabriel Pollard / the person behind the pages

Hello, I’m Gabriel.

The believer.
The father.
The writer.

A self-published author, believer and father of two. Gabriel writes to help readers meet changing times with discernment and confidence.

Get to know Gabriel +

Gabriel and his life partner are raising their two children with faith at the heart of their journey. His passion for writing began more than two decades ago.

Through life’s trials, he has returned to the page and to the belief that a higher power brings grounding, focus and perspective. He has written four self-published books. Hidden In Broad Daylight explores biblical prophecy and historical parallels.
